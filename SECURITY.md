# Security Policy

Abelian takes the security of its node, wallets and cryptographic libraries seriously. Thank you for helping keep the network and its users safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, pull requests or discussions.**

Report privately in one of these ways:

- **Preferred:** open the **Security** tab of the affected repository and click **Report a vulnerability**, where enabled (GitHub private vulnerability reporting).
- **Email or fallback:** security@pqabelian.io, with **"SECURITY"** in the subject line. Prefer GitHub private reporting for sensitive technical details; ordinary email is not an end-to-end encrypted reporting channel.

Please include the affected component and commit or version, a description and root cause, the impact, steps to reproduce (or a proof of concept that runs only on systems and local or private test networks you control), and how you would like to be credited.

We aim to acknowledge reports within **3 business days** and to give an initial assessment within **10 business days**.

## Rules

- **Do not exploit vulnerabilities on mainnet.** Test only on systems and local or private test networks you control. Testing on a public testnet or other live systems requires separate written authorisation for the specific activity from a party with authority over the affected systems.
- Do not access or keep other people's funds, keys or data.
- Coordinate disclosure and keep details confidential until the disclosure date determined under Section 6 of the full policy, including its notice and extension process.

Good-faith research that follows our policy is covered by our safe-harbour terms. Eligible reports may receive a discretionary reward.

**Full policy:** https://pqabelian.io/security
